Features That Help Every Farmer

Whether you use a smartphone or a simple button phone, BeFarm works for you.

📞

Toll-Free Slot Booking

No internet? No app? Just call our toll-free number to book your mandi slot. Works on any phone — even button phones.

📊

Live Mndi Prices

Check today's price of wheat, rice, cotton and other crops in real-time. Know the best price before you travel to mandi.

👥

Rush Indicator

See how crowded the mandi is right now. Plan your vist when it's less busy. Save 2-3 days of waiting time.

🔬

Digital Quality Testing

Moisture level and quality grade uploaded directly from testing machine. No inspector can change results manually. 100% tamper-proof.

🚫

No Middleman

The app replaces the middleman. You see prices, quality reports, and payment status yourself. No one takes your share.

💸

Payment Tracking

Track your payment status after selling crops. Know exactly when money will hit your bank account through Direct Benefit Transfer.

🌦️

Weather Alerts

Get warnings about thunderstorms, heavy rain before they happen. Protect your crop and plan transport on clear days.

🏪

Common Service Center

Can't book online? Visit your nearest Common Service Center (CSC). They'll book your slot and help you with everything.

📈

Operator Dashboard

Mandi operators can see how much crop is arriving today. Manage space, weighing scales, and staff efficiently with data.
